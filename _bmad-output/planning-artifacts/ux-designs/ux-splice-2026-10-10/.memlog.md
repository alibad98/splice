- (event) Sources: docs/prd.md v0.4, docs/prd-addendum.md, docs/tech-stack.md, architecture spine; demo slips-sage.vercel.app unreachable from sandbox. Stakes: consumer-facing B2B2C (Salla merchants + shoppers), bilingual AR/EN. Owner asks subagents at low effort; use impeccable + shadcn skills.
- (decision) Brand feel: energetic and social (creator-tool energy, Stories/Reels), tempered by admired references Linear/Vercel (crisp, precise) and Shopify Polaris/Stripe (clear commerce dashboards, strong data viz).
- (decision) Start fresh visually; demo is a feature/screen reference only.
- (decision) Theme: light first, full dark theme also defined.
- (decision) Form factors: merchant dashboard = responsive web (desktop/tablet first, usable on mobile), embedded in Salla dashboard iframe; storefront player = mobile-first overlay/inline in Salla themes. Bilingual AR (RTL) and EN (LTR). UI system: shadcn/ui on Radix + Tailwind 4 (from tech stack).
- (decision) Voice: friendly coach — warm, encouraging, short; celebrate wins briefly; errors explain what happened and the next step; no emoji.
- (decision) Language: Arabic first (RTL default); English available; designs drawn Arabic-first.
- (decision) Default storefront entry: story ring row on home/category pages, tap opens full-screen viewer (overlay). Per AD-8 the inline ring inserts only when its anchor is below the viewport, else falls back to overlay entry (floating bubble).
- (decision) Visual direction: Studio (owner pick). Electric violet primary #6A2BFF (dark #7A45FF), lime accent #C8F25A, pill buttons/badges, radius 16px (1rem), Plus Jakarta Sans 800 numerals + IBM Plex Sans Arabic, airy 14px base; revenue KPI tile as solid violet celebration block. Source: .working/direction-studio.html. Rejected: Signal (too austere for social energy), Ledger (least social).
- (override) Owner: no purples anywhere. Studio structure (pills, 16px radius, Plus Jakarta Sans 800 numerals, airy density, celebration tile) kept; violet primary and violet-tinted neutrals to be replaced.
- (decision) Primary colour: hot coral (~#FF4A3D) replaces violet; neutrals clean ink/grey (no purple tint); lime accent dropped in favour of warm coral-family accents; charts use distinct non-purple hues. Text-on-coral must meet AA (use ink text on bright coral or white on a deeper coral).
